# Saved-output verification

Run `python scripts/verify_outputs.py` to check the spatial-validation confusion matrix and pooled scores, the complete 12-month Lagos profile, and consistency of the independent VIIRS holdout CSV with its saved ROC AUC, average precision, Brier score, threshold metrics, geography, and time window. This is a read-only artifact check; it does not retrain the model or refetch Earth Engine observations. The full training pipeline fetches external historical inputs and can be expensive. The Nigeria spatial folds and later Lagos VIIRS holdout are separate evaluation tracks.
